# Editing your website

Everything a visitor reads or sees on the site can be changed from your editing panel, Pages CMS. Sign in at
https://app.pagescms.org with your GitHub account and open the `rooted-haus` project.

When you press **Save**, the site rebuilds itself and your change is live in about a minute. Nothing else to do.

## Where things are

The panel is ordered like the site's menu. Each "... page" entry holds that page's headings, text and photos; the
lists that appear on that page sit right after it.

| In the panel | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Studio details** | Name, booking link, the words on the booking buttons ("Get Rooted"), address, phone, hours, Instagram and Facebook, gift card link, and the lines in the footer. These show on every page. |
| **Seasonal deals** | The offer banner on Home and The Rituals. Each deal shows from its first day to its last day, then disappears by itself. |
| **Home page** | The slideshow (photos, the handwritten phrase on each, which side the words sit on), the new guest steps, the specialties, The Experience section, and the headings above the reviews, recent work and hiring band. |
| **Love letters** | The reviews. Tick "Show on the Home page" to feature one, and "Feature this one large" on just one of them. Reviews about a stylist also appear on that stylist's page. |
| **The Rituals page** and **Services & prices** | The top of the page and the photos beside the menu; then every service, price and time. |
| **Meet the Haus page** and **Team members** | The top of the page and the mission; then each person's name, role, photos, specialties, quote and bio. A new team member gets their own page automatically. |
| **The Experience page** and **The Experience groups** | The top of the page, the opening words and the closing note; then each group (A warm welcome, The rooted difference, The little extras): its photos and its list of points. |
| **Gallery page** and **Gallery photos** | The heading at the top, the buttons and the band under the photos; then each photo, its caption, its group (the filter buttons) and its size in the grid. |
| **Connect page** and **FAQ** | The top of the page and the headings for the message and newsletter forms (the forms appear once your web person has set up the form address in Studio details); then questions and answers (the FAQ section appears once there is at least one). |
| **Plant Your Roots page** | Everything on the careers page. The hourly roles you list also become choices in the application form. |

Buttons and small headings are editable too: each page entry has a short group for them (for example "Buttons at the
bottom of the page"). On Meet the Haus, `{name}` in one of those is replaced by the stylist's first name.

Every page also has **How the page appears in Google**: the title in the browser tab and in search results, and the
short summary underneath it. Keep the summary to about 150 characters.

## Headings

To set one phrase of a heading in the handwritten font, put a star on each side of it: `Make yourself *at home*`.
Keep it to two or three words, and to one phrase per heading.

## Photos

- **Top of each page:** the photo is always shown whole. A landscape photo (wider than tall) runs across the full screen with the heading set over its lower left corner (on phones the heading sits below it); a portrait photo sits beside the heading. Landscape looks best here. Leave the photo empty for a heading with no photo. On Meet the Haus the group photos sit beside the words instead, also shown whole.
- **Describe the photo** in a sentence, as if to someone who cannot see it ("A stylist curls a client's long blonde
  hair"). Screen readers read it aloud, and Google uses it too.
- **Other photos: crop position** decides which part of a photo stays in view when it is trimmed to fit its frame. Leave it empty to
  keep the center. To move it, type two numbers: across, then down. `50% 20%` keeps the upper middle (good for faces),
  `50% 80%` the lower middle.
- On the Home slideshow, pick **which side the words sit on**: the side of the photo without faces.

## If something goes wrong

If a change cannot be built (for example, a required box left empty), the site simply keeps showing the last working
version; nothing breaks for visitors. Undo the change in the panel, or ask your web person, who can see exactly what
happened.

Things that stay with your web person: the layout, colors, fonts, the menu, the footer's column titles, and the
labels on form boxes (Name, Email...). Ask whenever you want one of those changed.
