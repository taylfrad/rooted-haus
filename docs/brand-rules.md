# Brand rules

Source of truth: the "The Rooted Haus" design system project. Its tokens are copied into `web/src/styles/tokens/`; if the design changes there, update them here. The React components and website UI kit from that project are ported to Astro in `web/src/components/` and `web/src/pages/`.

Accessibility adjustments to the original tokens (measured against WCAG AA): muted text alpha .72 to .85; on-dark muted text alpha .75 to .9, with full ivory on olive; script "gold" `#BF7B3F` to `#A8662A`; focus ring is chocolate with an ivory halo instead of caramel; text buttons use chocolate; no caramel badge.

- **Color.** Seven logo colors plus derived espresso, linen and oat. Never pure black or white (client request). Sections alternate linen, ivory, olive, sand and espresso. Primary action is terracotta with ivory text; caramel is the "gold" accent.
- **Type.** Gilda Display for headlines (stand-in for Cassy Vogue), Montserrat for body and spaced capitals (stand-in for Gotham Pro), La Belle Aurore script (the owner's choice) for 2 to 5 lowercase words, at most one per screen area. Headlines in sentence case.
- **Shape.** Pill buttons and inputs, 28px cards, arch / circle / rounded photos. Never sharp rectangles. Flat color cards without borders; interactive cards lift 4px on hover.
- **Motion.** Every timing is a token in `web/src/styles/tokens/effects.css`; nothing is hard-coded in a page. Scroll reveals fade and rise 16px over 800ms (`--dur-reveal`, `--ease-glide`) with an 80ms stagger; hover changes take 320ms (`--dur-base`, `--ease-soft`); hero slides cross-fade over 1.2s (`--dur-slow`). A page change fades the old page out and the new one in with a 12px rise (`--dur-page-out`, `--dur-page`, `--rise-page`); a stylist's portrait glides from the team grid to their own page. Photos never slide or zoom on scroll; they only fade in once as they finish loading. No bounces, zooms or parallax. Respect reduced-motion.
- **Hover.** One of three shared classes from `global.css`, never a one-off: `.lift` (photos and cards rise 4px with a soft shadow), `.pill` (outlined pill fills chocolate), `.caps-link` (underline fades in). Buttons use the `Button` component.
- **Logo and frond.** The RH monogram is the site's recurring mark: whole above every page heading, as a small seal above a page's last button (`Seal`), and large and faint in the empty side margin of one or two sections per page on wide screens (`mark` on `Section`). Nothing is ever placed on top of it, so the faint one is sized to stay inside the margin. The frond (`Sprig`) appears only as the pair beside a page's closing words (`Flanked`), at most once per page, and never with loose leaves.
- **Icons.** Lucide, stroke 1.5, small and functional, often inside a 44px sand circle.
- **Voice.** "We", warm and a little poetic, more detail than punch. Buttons are 2 to 4 verbs ("Book an appointment"). No emoji, no hype words.
- **Photography.** Warm, natural light, slight sepia (`--photo-warmth`); never cool or black and white.
