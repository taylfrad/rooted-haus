Shelby has been in the industry for 11 years and 8 of them specializing in the art of blonding. She brings both experience and intention to her craft. Beyond the salon, she is a wife, mama to 2 boys with a 3rd little blessing on the way, and a dog mom to a golden retriever and a red lab.

She has a deep passion for and understanding of holistic living. Her passion is also deeply personal. After experiencing a vaccine injury as a child that left her with a lifelong heart condition, she eventually found herself on a journey to better understand her health and her body’s natural ability to heal and thrive. What began as a personal pursuit of wellness grew into a passion for intentional living, embracing whole, nourishing foods, natural remedies, and creating a low-tox environment for her family.

Over the years, that passion grew into a desire to share what she’d learned and encourage others to become more intentional about their own health and wellness. Eventually, she felt called to bring those same values into the beauty industry, creating a space where beautiful hair and a holistic lifestyle could coexist. And from that vision, The Rooted Haus was born.

But the roots of her vision run even deeper. Inspired by scripture she heard at church one Sunday, Colossians 2:6-7, Shelby believes that just as a plant needs strong roots to grow and flourish, we too need to be deeply rooted in Christ. Her faith became the foundation of The Rooted Haus, where beauty, wellness, and faith come together with purpose and harmony.
