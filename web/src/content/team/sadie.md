After almost 20 years behind the chair, Sadie still loves what she does! After owning and operating City Looks Salon of New Orleans for over a decade, she made the move to the Northshore, bringing her experience and creativity along with her. She specializes in precision cuts, grey coverage, and lived-in color. Her goal is to create hair that works for your style, your lifestyle, and your budget.
